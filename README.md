# Branding Wizard

**The AI creative director founders wish they could afford.**

Built for the founding team doing everything themselves. The PM writing the campaign
brief at 11pm. The marketer who keeps getting design back that "looks off" but can't
explain why. The startup that raised a seed round and still looks like a Webflow template.

---

## What It Is

Branding Wizard is a Claude skill that acts as a senior creative director — Pentagram
caliber thinking, YC-startup speed, none of the agency billing games.

It doesn't validate your ideas. It diagnoses them. It doesn't describe the direction —
it writes the brief, specifies the hex codes, drafts five taglines with strategic
rationale for each, tells you which competitor's visual language you accidentally copied
and how to get out from under it.

**It produces work you can hand to a designer or copywriter today.** Not strategy decks.
Not vision statements. Briefs.

## The Modes

| Mode | When | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | No brand, or one that never really existed | Full Brand DNA — tension, archetype, positioning, messaging, visual direction, AI image prompts |
| **Quick Brief** | You need a specific output fast | Taglines, color palettes, design briefs, campaign briefs — on demand |
| **Critique** | You share existing work | Named-heuristic diagnosis, commercial impact, specific fixes |
| **Campaign** | You're launching something | Campaign insight, creative concepts, channel plan, copy drafts |
| **Rebranding** | You're pivoting or outgrowing | Brand equity audit, what survives, transition architecture, new direction |

The skill auto-routes based on what you share. No command needed.

## What's Different

Most brand tools give you templates. This gives you thinking.

**Proprietary frameworks you won't find elsewhere:**

- **Brand Tension Matrix** — The insight that the most magnetic brands hold two opposing
  forces simultaneously. Apple's Simple/Powerful. Liquid Death's Healthy/Dangerous.
  Patagonia's Commercial/Environmental. The framework diagnoses your tension and explains
  the creative implications.

- **Category Villain Protocol** — Every great brand has an enemy — not a competitor,
  a behavior. Mailchimp's villain is stuffy email marketing. Stripe's villain is payment
  infrastructure as punishment. Naming yours creates advocates, not just customers.

- **The Specificity Ladder** — Forces brand language from "we're innovative" (worthless)
  to "AP that runs itself" (ownable). Five rungs. Nothing below Rung 4 ships.

- **The Onliness Statement** — "[Brand] is the only [category] that [differentiator] for
  [audience] who [need]." The operative word is "only." If you can't say only, you don't
  have a position.

- **14 Named Critique Heuristics** — The 5-Second Test, the Competitor Swap Test, the
  Brand Coherence Test, the Signature Moment Test, and 10 more. Named heuristics teach
  founders to see design problems — not just fix the one you showed them.

## How to Use It

Just talk to it like a creative director. It auto-routes.

**If you have nothing:**
> "I'm building [product] for [audience]. We have no brand yet. Help."

**If you need something specific:**
> "Write me 5 taglines for [product]. We're targeting [audience] and our competitors all say [common thing]."

**If something looks wrong:**
> "[upload screenshot] Why does this look generic?"

**If you're launching:**
> "We're launching our [feature/product] in 3 weeks. I need a campaign brief."

**If you're evolving:**
> "We just raised a Series A and need to look less like a scrappy startup without losing the community we built."

## The Design Philosophy

**Opinionated over safe.** It picks a direction and defends it. "It depends" is banned.

**Artifacts over essays.** Produces briefs, frameworks, and specs — not paragraphs of
advice that require you to figure out what to do with them.

**Specific over vague.** Says "#6366F1 electric indigo used only on interactive elements
and data callouts" not "a modern purple to add energy."

**Enemy-aware.** Good brands know what they're against. Good creative direction does too.
Every output names what we're NOT doing and why that matters.
