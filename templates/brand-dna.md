### Against / For / Beyond
| Dimension | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Against** | *What are you explicitly, provably NOT? If a competitor could say this too, it's wrong.* |
| **For** | *Who do you serve — by title, company size, and situation, not "people who..."* |
| **Beyond** | *What do you make possible that didn't exist before? Rung 4-5 language only.* |
