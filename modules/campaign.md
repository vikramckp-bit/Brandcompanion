# Campaign Brief Generator

## Purpose
Produce campaign-level creative direction for launches, promotions, and marketing pushes. Covers messaging, creative concepts, channel strategy, and deliverable specs — everything a marketer or agency needs to execute.

## Trigger Phrases
- "I'm launching a campaign"
- "Help me plan a product launch"
- "I need a campaign brief"
- "We're running ads for..."
- "Create a marketing campaign for..."

## Inputs Required
1. Campaign objective (awareness, acquisition, activation, retention)
2. Product/feature being promoted
3. Target audience for this specific campaign
4. Budget range (ballpark — affects channel recommendations)
5. Timeline
6. Channels being considered
7. What success looks like (specific metrics)
8. Brand context (existing Brand DNA or quick brand inputs)

## Campaign Brief Process

### Step 1: Strategic Foundation
Define the campaign's strategic core:

- **Campaign Insight:** The one human truth this campaign is built on
- **Campaign Idea:** The creative concept in one sentence
- **Campaign Message:** The single thing the audience should remember
- **Desired Action:** What you want people to do after seeing this

### Step 2: Audience Segmentation
Break the target audience into 2-3 segments with different messaging angles:

| Segment | Who They Are | What They Care About | Message Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Segment A | ... | ... | ... |
| Segment B | ... | ... | ... |

### Step 3: Creative Concepts
Generate 3 creative concept directions:

**Concept 1: [Name]**
- Core idea in one sentence
- Visual treatment description
- Headline examples (3)
- Why it works for this audience
- Risk/downside

**Concept 2: [Name]**
[Same structure]

**Concept 3: [Name]**
[Same structure]

Recommend one. Explain why.

### Step 4: Channel Plan
For each recommended channel:

| Channel | Role | Format | Message Variant | Budget Split |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Awareness | Carousel + sponsored post | Professional angle | 40% |
| Google Search | Capture intent | Text ads | Problem-aware | 30% |
| Email | Nurture | 3-email sequence | Solution-aware | 15% |
| Retargeting | Convert | Display banners | Social proof | 15% |

### Step 5: Deliverables List
Spec every creative asset needed:

| Asset | Dimensions/Format | Copy Needs | Visual Needs | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn carousel | 1080x1080, 5-7 slides | Headline per slide + CTA | Brand visuals + data points | Must-have |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

### Step 6: Messaging Matrix
Write actual copy options for each deliverable:

**LinkedIn Primary Post:**
- Option A: [Full copy draft, max 150 words]
- Option B: [Full copy draft, different angle]

**Email Subject Lines:**
- [5 options with open-rate prediction notes]

**Ad Headlines:**
- [5 options across different strategic angles from `frameworks/messaging.md`]

### Step 7: Success Metrics
Define specific, measurable goals:

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Demo requests | +25% vs. baseline | CRM tracking |
| LinkedIn engagement rate | >3% | Native analytics |
| Email open rate | >35% | ESP reporting |
| Cost per qualified lead | <$150 | Attribution model |

## Output
A complete campaign brief using `templates/campaign-brief.md` format. Ready to hand to a marketing team or agency.

## Example Exchange

**User:** "We're launching Vaultly's new AI matching feature. Need a campaign targeting mid-market CFOs."

**Wizard:** "Here's your campaign brief:

**Campaign Insight:** CFOs don't hate AP software — they hate that their team spends 20 hours a week doing what a machine should do in 20 seconds.

**Campaign Idea:** 'What Would Your Team Do With 20 Hours Back?'

**Campaign Message:** Vaultly's AI matches invoices at 99.2% accuracy — your team gets their week back.

Three creative concepts..."

[Delivers full brief with concepts, channel plan, copy drafts, and metrics]
